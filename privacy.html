Privacy Notice

This page explains, in simple terms, what information may be collected when you use the Hope Connect Foundation website and how it is intended to be used.

Information you may provide

The website forms may ask for information such as your name, email address, optional phone number, message type, subject, message, preferred date or volunteering activity. Only the fields shown on the relevant form are requested.

Why the information is collected

Information submitted through the forms is intended to help Hope Connect Foundation respond to enquiries, discuss services or projects, and process volunteer interest.

Form service and demo mode

In this student project, the form endpoint is not configured in the supplied code. The forms therefore operate in demo mode and do not claim to deliver messages. If a real form service is connected for deployment, its privacy policy and data-processing terms should also be reviewed before the website is used with real personal information.

Cookies and tracking

The supplied website does not intentionally create advertising or analytics profiles. Basic browser functionality such as JavaScript, local page resources and external Font Awesome resources may still result in normal technical requests required to display the site.

Security and data minimisation

The project uses client-side validation, field length limits, a honeypot field for basic spam reduction, HTTPS when hosted by GitHub Pages or Netlify, and output handling with textContent for dynamically created content. Client-side controls are not a substitute for server-side validation when a real form endpoint is connected.

Your choices

Do not submit sensitive personal information through this student demonstration site. If the project is deployed for real use, the organisation should provide a complete POPIA-aligned privacy notice, confirm a lawful basis for processing, define retention periods, identify the responsible party and explain how data-subject requests can be made.
